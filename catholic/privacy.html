Landfall Catholic is local-first. Its free reading, library, and note features do not require a Landfall login. Optional cloud features are described below.

What the Premium service retains

Landfall displays no advertising. Premium does not create a separate username or password. To verify an App Store purchase, prevent preview abuse, enforce allowances, and control spend, the service retains pseudonymous entitlement, installation-attestation, quota, request-status, feature-usage, and cost-accounting records. Feature-usage records identify the operation and allowance consumed, not the book, passage, prompt, or conversation content; they support allowance enforcement, service diagnostics, capacity planning, and aggregate usage statistics. It does not retain full books, notes, prompts, transcripts, request-specific narration audio, API keys, or raw Apple transaction identifiers in operational records. Published Landfall Studio audiobook files are public catalog assets, not user records.

What stays on your iPhone

Your books, margin notes, bookmarks, listening positions, podcast subscriptions, downloaded audio, and settings are stored on your device (and in your own iCloud device backup, under Apple's terms). Spoken notes are transcribed on-device using Apple's speech recognition. If you explicitly select Apple On-Device for book and podcast Jam, the supplied passage and conversation are processed by Apple's Foundation Models on your iPhone and are not sent to Landfall or OpenAI. Apple Premium narration voices are free system assets that run on-device after you download one in iPhone Settings. If you choose a published Landfall Studio audiobook, its public audio files stream from Landfall's content delivery service or download to your iPhone; those files contain no user data.

What leaves your iPhone, and when

  • Install and purchase measurement. Landfall uses Meta's iOS SDK to send an app activation event and limited device/app diagnostic information so we can measure whether an advertisement led to an install, and two further kinds of event so we can measure whether it led to a subscriber: when a Premium offer screen is shown (and which screen showed it), and your original Premium purchase — a free trial start or a paid subscription — with the plan's product id, its price and currency, and Apple's StoreKit transaction id for that purchase. Meta uses these events for analytics and advertising measurement. Renewals, plan changes, restores, refunds, and Family Sharing purchases are not sent. Landfall does not send Meta your books, notes, searches, podcasts, listening activity, prompts, conversations, name, email address, Apple ID, or other direct identifiers. Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt controls access to the advertising identifier and cross-app attribution. If you decline, Landfall keeps advertiser-ID collection disabled; Apple may still provide privacy-preserving aggregate campaign measurement. Landfall does not use Facebook Login.
  • Your own provider key — only if you add it and use an AI feature. Starting a Talk conversation streams your voice and the current passage to OpenAI; converting a book's reading level sends its text; note-tidying sends the note; and podcast discussion may send a short audio excerpt for transcription. Personal-key transcription happens directly between your device and OpenAI under OpenAI's API terms; we are not in the middle. Your key is stored in your iPhone's Keychain and is removable in the app at any time.
  • Landfall Premium — only when you choose a Premium operation. The text or audio needed for that operation is sent to Landfall and its AI processor. This can include a requested passage, conversion paragraphs, narration text, live-session audio, or a short podcast audio excerpt used to build a time-aligned transcript on your device. Podcast audio and transcript text are not retained by Landfall after that request. Live, request-specific narration audio is not retained after completion; this is separate from the published Studio audiobook catalog. A completed premium text reply can be stored for up to 24 hours so retrying the exact request returns the same answer instead of charging twice, then it is deleted automatically. Short-lived challenge/session records expire, while pseudonymous quota and cost records are retained for allowance enforcement and charge reconciliation.
  • Apple's podcast directory and each show's own RSS feed are fetched directly to browse and stream episodes.
  • The daily digest is a public, identical-for-everyone file. Reading it sends nothing but an ordinary web request.

What we never do

No ads inside Landfall, no selling of data, no sharing with data brokers, and no advertising fingerprinting. Meta measurement is limited to the activation, offer-screen, and original-purchase events described above and is never given reading or conversation content.

Children

Landfall Catholic is not directed at children and is rated accordingly on the App Store.
