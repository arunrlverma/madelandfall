Common questions

Do I need an account?
No. There is nothing to sign up for.
Do I need an OpenAI key?
No. Listening, voice notes, podcasts, and the daily digest all work without one. A key (from platform.openai.com) unlocks premium narration, Talk conversations, reading levels, note tidying, and offline audio. Add or remove it in the reader's ⋯ menu.
Where are my notes?
Inside the book, pinned to the line where you spoke them — open the Notes panel from the reader, or export the whole book with notes to Word.
Playback stopped in a tunnel.
It reconnects automatically and resumes where you were. Tapping play retries immediately.
How do I delete my data?
Delete the app. Everything is on your device; nothing remains anywhere else. To remove just your OpenAI key: ⋯ menu → Settings.
